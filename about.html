What is ASC Adventures?

ASC Adventures is a continuation of AFC Adventures (basically AFC Adventures 3), which you can check out on my website here.

Why "ASC Adventures"?

Since Another Furry Convention (AFC) was rebranded to Anthro SoCal (ASC), I decided to follow suit and rebrand to ASC Adventures. Don't worry, things will still be the same!
