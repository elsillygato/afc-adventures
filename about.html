About ASC Adventures!

What is ASC Adventures?

ASC Adventures is a continuation of AFC Adventures (basically AFC Adventures 3), which you can check out on my website here.

Why "ASC Adventures"?

Since Another Furry Convention (AFC) was rebranded to Anthro SoCal (ASC), I decided to follow suit and rebrand to ASC Adventures. Don't worry, things will still be the same!

The Lore (Thus Far)

After Lord Gato and his crew defeated Dragith during AFC2024, they tried to return to their home at the hideout. However, the portal they used to get to the Freenet Kingdom had been destroyed by a surviving minion. To repair the portal, the crew would need to obtain various materials to rebuild it, starting with some minerals obtained by defeating Dodonga at AFC2025.

After the defeat of Dodonga, the crew needed to obtain some Ka-Ching, which is offered as a prize at the upcoming Beast Brawl (from ASC2026).
